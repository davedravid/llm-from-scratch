Once upon a time there was a king who lived in a big castle. The king had a kind queen and a young prince. Every morning the prince walked in the garden with his dog. One day the dog found a golden key under a tree. The prince gave the key to the king, and the king smiled.

Once upon a time a little girl lived near the forest. She had a red hat and a small basket. Every day she took bread to her grandmother. One morning she saw a fox on the path. The fox was friendly, and they walked to the house together.

Once upon a time there was a wise old owl. The owl lived in a tall tree in the forest. All the animals came to ask the owl for help. One night a lost rabbit came to the tree. The owl showed the rabbit the way home.

There was once a princess who loved to sing. She sang in the castle every evening. The queen said her voice was like a bird. One day a bird flew into the castle and sang with her. The princess and the bird became best friends.

Once upon a time a small cat lived on a farm. The cat liked to sleep in the warm sun. Every day the farmer gave the cat some milk. One day the cat saw a mouse in the barn. The cat and the mouse decided to be friends.

Once upon a time there was a brave knight. The knight rode a white horse to the castle. The king asked the knight to find the lost crown. The knight looked in the forest and in the river. At last he found the crown in a dark cave.

A young boy lived in a small house by the sea. Every morning he walked on the beach. He liked to look for shells in the sand. One day he found a shell that could sing. He took the shell home and gave it to his mother.

Once upon a time there was a happy dog named Max. Max loved to run in the park. Every day he played with a red ball. One day the ball rolled into the lake. A kind duck pushed the ball back to Max.

There was once a wicked old witch who lived in a dark tower. The witch did not like the people of the village. One day she cast a spell on the river. A clever girl found a way to break the spell. The river was clean again, and the village was happy.

Once upon a time a little bear lived in the forest. The bear loved to eat honey. One day the bear found a tree full of bees. The bees were angry, so the bear ran away. Later the bees gave the bear a small pot of honey.

Once upon a time there was a queen who ruled a big kingdom. The queen was kind and fair to everyone. One winter the people had no food. The queen opened the castle doors and shared her bread. The people loved their queen even more.

A small bird lived in a nest at the top of a tree. The bird was afraid to fly. Every day her mother said, you can do it. One sunny morning the bird jumped from the nest. She flew over the forest and sang a happy song.

Once upon a time there was a prince who wanted to see the world. He left the castle with a map and a bag of bread. He walked through the forest and over the hills. He met a farmer, a baker and a fisherman. When he came home, he told the king about everything he saw.

Once upon a time a little girl had a magic garden. The flowers in the garden could talk. Every morning the girl said hello to the flowers. One day a rose said it was thirsty. The girl gave the rose water, and the rose thanked her.

There was once a big green dragon who lived on a mountain. The people of the village were afraid of the dragon. One day a brave girl climbed the mountain. She found that the dragon was lonely and sad. The girl and the dragon became friends, and the village was not afraid anymore.

Once upon a time a rabbit and a turtle had a race. The rabbit was very fast and the turtle was very slow. The rabbit ran ahead and took a nap under a tree. The turtle kept walking and did not stop. The turtle won the race, and the rabbit learned a lesson.

Once upon a time there was a kind king who loved books. The king built a big library in the castle. Every child in the kingdom could come to read. One day a young girl read every book in the library. The king made her the keeper of the books.

A little mouse lived in a hole in the wall. Every night the mouse looked for cheese in the kitchen. One night the mouse found a big piece of cheese. The mouse was too small to carry it. Her friends came to help, and they shared the cheese together.

Once upon a time there was a young prince who was very shy. The prince did not like to talk to people. One day he met a talking frog by the pond. The frog told the prince funny stories every day. Soon the prince was not shy anymore.

Once upon a time a family of ducks lived by a river. The smallest duck could not swim well. Every day his mother helped him practice. One day there was a race on the river. The smallest duck swam very fast and won the race.

There was once a poor farmer who had a magic hen. Every morning the hen laid a golden egg. The farmer sold the eggs and bought food for his family. One day a greedy man tried to steal the hen. The hen flew away, and the greedy man got nothing.

Once upon a time a little star lived in the sky. The star wanted to visit the earth. One night the star fell into a field. A kind girl found the star and kept it warm. The next night the girl helped the star go back to the sky.

Once upon a time there was a queen who had a beautiful garden. In the garden there was a tree with silver apples. Every year the queen gave the apples to the children of the kingdom. One year a storm broke the tree. The children planted a new tree for the queen.

A young girl lived in a small village by the forest. She wanted to be a knight like her father. Every day she practiced with a wooden sword. One day a wolf came to the village. The girl was brave and chased the wolf away.

Once upon a time there was a lazy cat who did not want to work. The other animals on the farm worked all day. In the winter the lazy cat had no food. The kind dog shared his food with the cat. After that, the cat helped on the farm every day.

Once upon a time there was a princess who lived in a tall tower. She was not sad, because she loved to paint. Every day she painted the forest, the river and the sky. One day a prince saw her paintings and asked to learn. The princess taught the prince to paint, and they painted together.

There was once an old man who lived alone in the forest. Every day he made toys out of wood. He gave the toys to the children of the village. The children loved the old man and visited him often. The old man was never lonely again.

Once upon a time a little fish lived in a big blue sea. The fish wanted to see the world above the water. One day a friendly bird took the fish to the shore. The fish saw the sand, the trees and the sun. Then the fish went home and told the other fish about the world.

Once upon a time there was a king who lost his golden ring. He looked in every room of the castle. The queen looked in the garden, and the prince looked in the kitchen. At last the little princess found the ring in her shoe. The king laughed and gave her a big hug.

Once upon a time a small boy wanted a pet dog. His mother said he had to be kind and patient. Every day the boy fed the birds in the garden. One day a little lost dog came to the garden. The boy took care of the dog, and the dog stayed with him forever.
